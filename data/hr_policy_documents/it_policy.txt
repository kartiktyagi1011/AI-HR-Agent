AURA TECHNOLOGIES INC. - HR POLICY MANUAL
DOCUMENT REF: POL-2026-IT-005
SUBJECT: ACCEPTABLE USE OF IT ASSETS & DATA SECURITY
EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2026

1. PROPRIETARY DEVICES & SOFTWARE
Company-issued laptops, mobiles, and software licenses are exclusively for corporate business. Installation of unauthorized third-party executable files or unvetted AI tools (e.g., public LLMs with company code) is prohibited.

2. CONFIDENTIAL DATA HANDLING
Customer PII, source code, and strategic financial plans must never be stored on personal cloud storage accounts (e.g., personal Google Drive, Dropbox).

3. INCIDENT REPORTING
Any lost hardware or suspected security breach/phishing incident must be reported to security@auratech.com within 2 hours.

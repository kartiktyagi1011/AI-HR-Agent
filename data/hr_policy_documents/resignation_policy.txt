AURA TECHNOLOGIES INC. - HR POLICY MANUAL
DOCUMENT REF: POL-2026-RSG-008
SUBJECT: RESIGNATION, NOTICE PERIODS & SEPARATION
EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2026

1. NOTICE PERIODS
- IC & Specialist Level: 2 weeks notice.
- Management & Senior Roles: 4 weeks notice.
- Executive Officers: 60 days notice.

2. ASSET RETURN & EXIT INTERVIEW
All corporate property (laptop, badges, corporate cards) must be returned on or before the final working day. Final paycheck will be disbursed in accordance with state regulations.

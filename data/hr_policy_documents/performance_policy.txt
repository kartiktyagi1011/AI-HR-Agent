AURA TECHNOLOGIES INC. - HR POLICY MANUAL
DOCUMENT REF: POL-2026-PRF-006
SUBJECT: PERFORMANCE APPRAISAL & PIP PROCESS
EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2026

1. EVALUATION CYCLES
Performance is evaluated bi-annually (Mid-Year in July, Year-End in December) using a 5-point scale (1: Unsatisfactory to 5: Exceptional).

2. PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN (PIP)
- Employees receiving a rating of "1" or two consecutive ratings of "2" will be placed on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
- Weekly check-ins with management and HR are mandatory during the PIP period. Failure to meet PIP targets will lead to employment termination.

AURA TECHNOLOGIES INC. - HR POLICY MANUAL
DOCUMENT REF: POL-2026-PAY-007
SUBJECT: PAYROLL DISTRIBUTION, OVERTIME & COMMISSIONS
EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2026

1. PAY SCHEDULE
Salaries are paid semi-monthly on the 15th and last business day of each month via direct deposit.

2. OVERTIME COMPLIANCE
Non-exempt employees working beyond 40 hours per week are eligible for 1.5x overtime pay. All overtime must be pre-approved in writing by the department manager.

3. SALES COMMISSION INCENTIVES
Commissions are calculated on closed-won revenue confirmed in Salesforce by 11:59 PM on the last calendar day of the month and paid in the subsequent month's payroll cycle.

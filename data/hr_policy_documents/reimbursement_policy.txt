AURA TECHNOLOGIES INC. - HR POLICY MANUAL
DOCUMENT REF: POL-2026-EXP-004
SUBJECT: CORPORATE TRAVEL & EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2026

1. EXPENSE SUBMISSION DEADLINES
All expense claims must be submitted with valid tax receipts via Concur within 30 days of incurring the expense. Claims submitted after 60 days will be rejected.

2. TRAVEL MEALS & INCIDENTALS
- Daily meal allowance (Per Diem): Up to $75/day (Itemized receipts required for meals exceeding $25).
- Alcohol purchases are non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized client entertainment event approved by a VP.

3. HARDWARE & INTERNET SUBSIDIES
- Remote employees receive a monthly $75 broadband allowance.
- Home office setup stipend ($500 one-time) is reimbursable upon joining.

AURA TECHNOLOGIES INC. - HR POLICY MANUAL
DOCUMENT REF: POL-2026-WFH-003
SUBJECT: HYBRID & REMOTE WORK POLICY
EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2026

1. HYBRID ELIGIBILITY
- Standard hybrid roles require 3 days of in-office presence (Tuesday through Thursday) and 2 flexible remote days.
- Fully remote status requires executive committee approval and a formal Remote Work Agreement.

2. INTERNATIONAL REMOTE WORK RESTRICTIONS
- Working remotely from an international location is strictly limited to 14 business days per calendar year and requires 30 days prior written authorization from HR, Legal, and Tax Compliance.
- Unapproved international work will result in disciplinary action due to permanent establishment tax risks and data privacy laws (GDPR/CCPA).

3. ERGONOMIC & DATA SECURITY OBLIGATIONS
- Employees must maintain a quiet, secure workspace. Public Wi-Fi for work tasks must be accessed exclusively through the corporate VPN.

AURA TECHNOLOGIES INC. - HR POLICY MANUAL
DOCUMENT REF: POL-2026-LEV-002
SUBJECT: PAID TIME OFF (PTO), SICK LEAVE & STATUTORY LEAVE
EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2026

1. ANNUAL PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
- Full-time employees accrue 20 business days of annual leave per calendar year (accrued at 1.66 days per month).
- Maximum carryover into the next calendar year is 5 days. Any excess unaccrued PTO beyond 5 days is forfeited on December 31.
- Planned leave exceeding 3 consecutive days requires 14 calendar days prior notice via AuraHR.

2. SICK & MEDICAL LEAVE
- Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Medical certificates are required for absences extending beyond 2 consecutive business days.

3. PARENTAL & FAMILY LEAVE
- Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid Parental Leave.
- Secondary caregivers are eligible for 6 weeks of fully paid Parental Leave.
- Requires submission of birth or adoption documentation at least 30 days prior to commencement.
